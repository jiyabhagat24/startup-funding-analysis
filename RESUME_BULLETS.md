The dataset has 2,818 funding rounds from 1,747 startups, so "10k+ records" would not be accurate.

**Global Startup Funding Analysis & Prediction** | Python, Pandas, NumPy, Matplotlib, Seaborn, Scikit-learn, Streamlit
- Analyzed 2,800+ funding rounds from 1,700+ startups across 35 countries and 20 sectors using Pandas and NumPy, including data-quality checks that flagged 700+ rounds dated before the startup's founding year.
- Built 12 exploratory analyses and visualizations with Seaborn and Matplotlib covering funding by stage, country, sector, region, investor type and startup traits.
- Developed a Random Forest model (grouped train/test split by startup) to predict high-funding rounds: 0.92 ROC AUC and 81% accuracy on held-out startups, with a funding-stage-only baseline to show what the other features add.
- Built an interactive Streamlit dashboard to explore funding trends by country and industry, with filters, a country-by-industry heatmap and a live prediction form.
